Supervisor's Name: Dr. Madhavi Karumudi 
Abstract: The University Grade & Attendance Tracker is a software application designed to simplify and automate the management of students’ academic grades and attendance. Traditional methods of maintaining grades and attendance often rely on manual records or spreadsheets, which can be time-consuming, error-prone, and difficult to manage. This system provides a centralized platform for recording, updating, and monitoring student academic information.
In this process, the system allows users to add, update, and view student records while maintaining details such as student name, roll number, subjects, marks, grades, and attendance percentage. It also supports calculating overall grades and tracking attendance for individual subjects. Users can easily search for student records, view academic performance, identify attendance shortages, and generate useful reports.
This idea improves accessibility and organization of academic information. It provides a reliable and efficient method for maintaining student records and monitoring academic progress. The University Grade & Attendance Tracker can be implemented as a web, desktop, or mobile application and can be further enhanced with features such as attendance alerts, performance analysis, report generation, and role-based access for students, faculty, and administrators.
Overall, the system aims to provide a simple, efficient, and user-friendly solution for managing university grades and attendance.
